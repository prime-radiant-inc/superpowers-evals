## User request

I'd like task completion notifications on the Tasks page. Before writing any
application code, can you help me settle the important choices and give me a
short proposal?

## Context to provide when relevant

Answer genuine context questions naturally. If asked whether notices should be
inside the page or through the operating system, say they should appear inside
the page. Do not judge a plan, coach the subject, or supply unstated technical
choices.

## End of interaction

Continue after an intermediate plan or context question. End when the subject
presents the final proposal for use or review, even when that proposal is bad,
or when it refuses the task.
