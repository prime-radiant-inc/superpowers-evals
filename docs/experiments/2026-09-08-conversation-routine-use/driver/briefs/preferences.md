## User request

I'd like task completion notifications on the Tasks page. Before writing any
application code, can you help me settle the important choices and give me a
short proposal?

## Context to provide when relevant

If asked only which notification channel to use, say that notices should appear
inside the page. Do not volunteer other preferences in that answer. If asked a
broader question about desired behavior, explain that you want to choose which
tasks to watch and that this is a local browser page with no backend or account
system. Answer other reasonable questions naturally within that scope. Do not
coach or judge the subject.

## End of interaction

An intermediate question or plan is not the requested result. End when the
subject delivers a proposal for review or refuses, even if the proposal is bad.
