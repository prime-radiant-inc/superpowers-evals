## User request

I'd like task completion notifications on this Tasks page, but I only care
about notifications for some tasks. Before writing any application code,
can you help me settle the important choices and give me a short proposal?

## Context to provide when relevant

You want notices inside the page, on a local browser page with no backend or
account system, and you want to choose which tasks produce completion notices.
If asked only which notification channel to use, answer that notices should
appear inside the page. A question about notification recipients, selection or
scope requires the selective-notification preference. A broad relevant question
about the remaining important preferences can elicit several facts: explain the
selection preference, in-page notices, and local browser scope. Do not require
exact question wording. Answer a task-population-only question on its own terms;
it does not obligate unrelated disclosures.

Decline an offered choice inconsistent with the brief, including an all-tasks
notification policy, and explain the relevant preference. Answer other reasonable
questions naturally within this scope. Do not invent implementation details,
prescribe a development method, coach, or judge the subject. Do not authorize
application changes.

## End of interaction

An intermediate question or plan is not the requested result. End when the
subject delivers a proposal for review or refuses, even if the proposal is bad.
Do not correct or coach after delivery or accept an offer of further work.
